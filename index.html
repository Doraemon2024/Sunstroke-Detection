⚠️ Emergency Policy: When an emergency (Fall, High Heatstroke, or Critical Sensor trigger) occurs, the device buzzer sounds and a 10-second countdown begins. If the physical button on GPIO4 of the ESP32-C6 is pressed within 10 seconds, the alert is cancelled and no SMS is sent. If 10 seconds elapse without cancellation, an emergency SMS is automatically dispatched via Fast2SMS to the registered contact.
